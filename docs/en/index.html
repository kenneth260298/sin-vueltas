The problem

Getting paperwork done shouldn't take three trips.

The information is scattered

Requirements are spread across dozens of pages and PDFs from different institutions.

It depends on your situation

Being salaried, self-employed or retired changes the rules, and it's rarely clear which ones apply to you.

One missing document, one more trip

Showing up without a document means booking another appointment and coming back another day.

How it works

Answers that come from the official source.

The assistant is designed to answer only with what official documents say, and only from versions a person has already reviewed.

  1. 1

    Reads official sources

    Downloads pages and PDFs from public institutions, following each site's rules.

  2. 2

    A person reviews

    Every new version is compared with the previous one and approved before it enters the system.

  3. 3

    Searches by meaning

    Finds what you mean even if you don't use the institution's exact words.

  4. 4

    Answers with the source

    Gives you the requirements for your profile, with the official link so you can check.

Trust

Non-negotiable rules.

A paperwork assistant has to be reliable before it is clever.

  • Uses only official sources and always tells you where each fact came from.
  • Never asks for or stores your ID number.
  • Doesn't recommend banks or financial products.
  • Follows each website's rules and identifies itself when it visits.
  • Treats page text as information, never as instructions, to guard against prompt injection.
